// FAQ

Questions we get
all the time

How much revenue am I actually losing right now?
+
More than most founders realise. Research consistently shows that between 60 and 65% of B2B buying decisions involve after-hours research — evenings, early mornings, and weekends, when your team is offline.

📊 62% of purchase decisions involve after-hours or weekend activity

Without a closer available at those moments, visitors land, browse, hit a question and leave. Forever. The average website converts under 2% of its traffic, meaning 98 out of every 100 people you paid to acquire never become customers.
How is Converge different from Intercom or Drift?
+
Intercom and Zendesk are built for customer support — answering tickets, routing to agents. They wait for someone to ask a question and then try to answer it.

Drift was acquired by Salesloft in 2024 and now sits inside an enterprise sales platform priced at $2,500/month minimum.

Converge is built for revenue generation: proactive conversations, qualification, objection handling, and conversion — without human involvement.

💡 Intercom starts at $29/seat/mo. Drift starts at $2,500/mo. Converge starts free.
Will it sound robotic?
+
No, if you train it well. And we make that easy.

During setup, you define your brand voice, tone, and response style. Upload existing sales scripts, FAQs, and example conversations from your best reps. The result sounds like your company.
What happens when someone wants to talk to a real person?
+
Converge knows when to step aside. When a visitor requests a human, or when the AI detects a high-intent lead, it routes to your team via Slack, email, or your CRM with the full conversation context attached.
How does the free plan work?
+
The free plan gives you 50 AI conversations per month. No credit card, no time limit. Included: 1 AI persona, lead capture, email notifications, and basic analytics.

💡 Most teams upgrade within 2 weeks after seeing the lift firsthand.
What does "1.5% of attributed revenue" mean on the Growth plan?
+
It means our incentives are aligned with yours. We only charge the performance percentage on revenue we can demonstrably prove Converge helped generate.

The $149/mo base covers platform access. If you're converting well, the 1.5% is a fraction of what you're gaining.
Where did the name "Converge" come from?
+
Two things need to meet in every sale: a visitor with a problem, and a product that solves it. Most websites let those two things pass each other in the night.

Converge is that moment they actually meet.
