Questions

Things people ask
before they get started

How is Converge different from Intercom or generic chatbots?
+
Intercom is built for customer support — answering tickets and routing to agents. Generic chatbots wait to be asked a question and then try to answer it.

Converge is built for revenue generation. The specific difference is inquiry-based closing logic: when a visitor describes a problem, Converge treats it as the reason they arrived and moves immediately to qualification and booking. It does not apologise for their situation. It closes it.
Will it sound robotic or put visitors off?
+
Not if it is set up correctly. During installation, we read your site and generate a persona that matches your brand voice. You review and approve before it goes live.

The closing logic sounds like a knowledgeable member of your team, not a scripted bot. It references specifics from your site and responds to what visitors actually say.
What does installation actually involve?
+
One script tag before your closing body tag. No backend access, no CMS credentials, no developer required. Works with Webflow, WordPress, Shopify, custom HTML, or any platform that lets you add HTML to the page.

If you need help, we walk you through it or do it ourselves on the Managed plan.
What happens when someone wants a real person?
+
Converge either books a call directly into your calendar, or routes the conversation to your team via email or Slack with the full conversation context attached. Your team jumps in already knowing what the prospect wants, what they have been told, and where they are in the decision.
Is there a free trial?
+
We offer a 7-day pilot on the Growth plan so you can see real results before committing. Enter your URL in the hero above — Converge reads your site in about 10 seconds and opens with a message specific to your business. No signup required for that.
