FAQ

Common questions

How is Converge different from Intercom or Drift?
+
Intercom and Zendesk are built for customer support — answering tickets, routing to agents. Drift was acquired by Salesloft in 2024 and now starts at $2,500 per month with an annual contract.

Converge is built for revenue generation: proactive conversations, qualification, objection handling, and conversion without human involvement. The specific difference is inquiry-based closing logic: when a visitor describes a problem, Converge qualifies and books rather than empathising and waiting.
Will it sound robotic or put visitors off?
+
Not if it's set up correctly. During installation, we read your site and generate a persona that matches your brand voice. You review and approve before it goes live.

The closing logic is designed to sound like a knowledgeable member of your team, not a scripted bot. It references specifics from your site and responds to what visitors actually say.
What does installation actually involve?
+
One script tag before your closing body tag. No backend access, no CMS credentials, no developer required. Works with Webflow, WordPress, Shopify, custom HTML, or any platform that allows you to add HTML to the page.

If you have a developer, it's a two-minute job. If you don't, we walk you through it, or do it for you on the Managed plan.
What happens when someone wants to speak to a real person?
+
Converge either books a call directly into your calendar, or routes the conversation to your team via email or Slack with the full conversation context attached. Your team jumps in already knowing what the prospect wants, what they've been told, and where they are in the decision.
Is there a free trial?
+
We offer a 7-day pilot on the Growth plan so you can see real results before committing to a monthly subscription. Use the live demo above first — enter your URL and watch Converge read your site and open with a message specific to your business. No signup required for that.
